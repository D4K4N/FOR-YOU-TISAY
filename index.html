FROM: YOUR KUYA RAM

Happy Birthday, gwapings nga akong pamangkin!

Dali ra kaayo ang panahon, no? It feels like only yesterday when you were younger, and now, look at you—you've grown into such a beautiful and strong young woman.

1

Seeing you shine today brings happy tears to my eyes. I am beyond proud of the person you are becoming, and I feel so blessed to have you as my pamangkin.

And honestly, hahahaha, while writing this, nakahilak ko, and I don't even know why. Maybe it's just overwhelming to realize how much you've grown and how grateful I am to have you in my life.

2

As you grow older, I hope you never lose the beautiful things that make you who you are. Padayon lang sa imong pagka-masinabtanon, pagka-buotan, pagka-madasigon sa pag-alagad sa Ginoo, ug pagka-gwapa, inside and out.

Always remember that you deserve all the happiness, love, and beautiful things this world has to offer.

3

Ayaw gyud kawad-i og paglaom, Tisay. I know life will not always be easy, and there will be days when things feel heavy, uncertain, and exhausting.

But please, take your time. Antos lang sa kung unsay naa karon, one day at a time.

4

Never let your present circumstances make you question the beautiful future that is waiting for you. You have so much ahead of you, and I genuinely believe that better days are coming.

And now that you're turning 20, I know you're slowly entering a new chapter of your life.

5

You're legally an adult now, but please remember that growing older doesn't mean you have to rush into everything that comes with adulthood.

Dili tungod kay 20 na ka, kinahanglan na nimo sundon ang tanan nga ginabuhat sa uban.

6

Please, ayaw padala sa impluwensya sa uban, especially when it comes to drinking, getting drunk, or doing things just because everyone else is doing them.

Learn to enjoy life without compromising your safety, your values, and the person you've worked so hard to become. You don't have to prove anything to anyone.

7

And one more thing, Tisay, sometimes when our guardians, parents, or elders remind us, correct us, or stop us from doing certain things, I hope you learn to understand where they're coming from.

Dili tanan nga pagbadlong kay pagpugong sa atong kalipay.

8

Sometimes, it's their way of protecting us from things we may not yet understand. They have their reasons, and most of the time, those reminders come from love, concern, and the desire to keep us safe.

9

Even me, as your kuya, if there are times when I remind you or tell you not to do something, bisan usahay madala ta sa atong mga kabug-at sa dughan, kasuko, o mga problema, please know that it is never because I want to limit your happiness.

10

It's because I care about you, and I want to see you grow without having to learn every lesson the hard way.

Someday, I hope you'll understand that some of the reminders we give you are simply our way of saying, "We love you, and we don't want anything bad to happen to you."

11

Tisay, thank you for being part of some of the most meaningful moments of my life. I will always treasure the fact that you were there beside me during my graduation and baccalaureate.

Maybe those moments seemed simple to you, but they meant so much to me. Having you there made those milestones even more special. I hope you know that your presence in my life is something I will never take for granted.

12

As your kuya, I want you to know that I will always be here to support you, cheer for you, and believe in you, even on days when you find it difficult to believe in yourself.

Please never forget that no matter how old you get, how far you go, or how heavy life becomes, you will always have me in your corner. You don't have to face everything alone.

13

Ug palihog, kung naa kay kinahanglan sa akoa, ayaw gyud kaulaw og duol or pagpangayo og tabang. You never have to hesitate or feel like you're bothering me.

If there's anything I can do to make things a little easier for you, I will gladly do it. I may not always have the perfect words or all the answers, but I hope my presence will always remind you that you have a kuya who genuinely cares for you.

14

And honestly, I don't know why, but there's a different kind of happiness I feel every time I get to see you. Maybe it's because, no matter how much time passes, karon dalaga na kaayu, and I will always find joy in seeing you happy, safe, and doing well.

15

One day, life may take us to different places. We may become busier, have our own responsibilities, and spend less time together.

But I hope you never outgrow the thought that you can always come to your kuya—not just when life is difficult, but also when you have good news to share, dreams to celebrate, or simply stories you want someone to listen to.

16

If there's one thing I wish for you, it is that life becomes kinder to you. I hope you achieve the dreams you quietly pray for, find the happiness you truly deserve, and become the woman you've always dreamed of being.

And when that day comes, I hope you will look back and remember that someone has always been proud of you, even long before the world gets to see everything you are capable of.

17

And Tisay, if there's one thing I hope you never forget, it's that having you as my pamangkin is one of the blessings I will forever thank God for.

I may not always say it, and I may not always show it perfectly, but please know that a part of my heart will always be proud of you.

18

I wish I could protect you from every pain this world may bring, but I know I can't. That's why I always include you in my prayers, every single day. I pray that God guides your steps, protects your heart, keeps you away from harm, and grants the dreams you've been quietly praying for.

Even in moments when I can't be by your side, I hope you find comfort knowing that somewhere, your kuya is praying for you.

19

No matter how old we get, you will always be that pamangkin I will worry about, pray for, and love for the rest of my life.

Happy birthday once again, Tisay. Thank you for existing, for being part of my life, and for giving me another reason to be proud and grateful in this life.

20

I love you more than words could ever explain. And if there's one thing I want you to carry with you wherever life takes you, it is this: You will never have to earn your place in my life.

You are my pamangkin, and I will always be your kuya. Through every season, every struggle, and every chapter of your life, I will always be here.

21

Enjoy your special day, gwapings. You deserve all the love in the world.

Happy Birthday, Duday Tisay! — From Kuya Ram
